# Wiring Scaffold

Use JC for eight 3.3 V logic inputs and JD for the Zynq UART0 EMIO link with
RTS/CTS. GPIO numbers and ESP32 module-specific mappings remain provisional
until the exact module and board schematic are confirmed. Do not connect JC to
5 V signals. Physical wiring and flash/program actions are `[HUMAN]` gates.
